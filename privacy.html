1. Introduction

Welcome to ReelHub ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile and web application (the "Service").

By using ReelHub, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the Service.

This policy applies to all users of ReelHub, regardless of location. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use ReelHub, you may provide:

  • Account Information: Your name, username, email address, and password
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, bio, and other details you choose to add
  • Content: Videos, photos, comments, messages, and stories you upload or share
  • Communications: Direct messages, group chat messages, and voice messages
  • Feedback: Any information you provide when contacting support or reporting issues

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you use ReelHub, we automatically collect some information:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, and version
  • Usage Data: Videos watched, likes, follows, comments, and interaction patterns
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, error logs, and pages visited
  • Location Data: General location based on IP address (we do not collect precise GPS location)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

3.1 Core Features

  • Create and manage your ReelHub account
  • Display your content to other users based on your privacy settings
  • Enable communication features like chats, groups, and comments
  • Send notifications about activity related to your account
  • Provide personalized content recommendations

3.2 Safety & Security

  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms
  • Investigate reports and take action against policy violations
  • Protect the safety of our users and community
  • Comply with legal obligations and law enforcement requests

3.3 Improvement & Support

  • Improve ReelHub's features and user experience
  • Analyze usage patterns to make better decisions
  • Respond to your support requests and feedback
  • Send you important updates about the Service

4. What We Don't Do

✅ Our Commitment to You:
  • ❌ We never sell your personal information to third parties
  • ❌ We never share your password with anyone (it's encrypted)
  • ❌ We don't read your private messages
  • ❌ We don't track your activity outside ReelHub
  • ❌ We don't use third-party advertising trackers
  • ❌ We don't access your camera or microphone without permission

5. Sharing Your Information

5.1 With Other Users

Your profile information (name, username, profile photo, and bio) is visible to other ReelHub users. Your posts, stories, and comments are visible based on your account privacy settings:

  • Public Account: Anyone on ReelHub can see your content
  • Private Account: Only approved followers can see your content

5.2 With Service Providers

We share data with trusted service providers who help us run ReelHub. These providers:

  • Only access data needed to perform their specific services
  • Are bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Must comply with applicable data protection laws

5.3 For Legal Reasons

We may share your information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of ReelHub, our users, or others. This includes cooperating with law enforcement when necessary.

7. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement multiple layers of protection:

  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard encryption
  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Access to user data is strictly limited to authorized personnel
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability testing
  • Automatic logout from inactive sessions
  • Two-factor authentication support (recommended)
⚠️ Important Notice: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data using commercially acceptable means, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your account credentials private.

8. Data Retention

We keep your personal data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile is immediately removed from ReelHub
  • Your posts, stories, and comments are deleted
  • Some data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days
  • Data required for legal or tax compliance may be retained longer
  • Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained for analytics

10. Children's Privacy

ReelHub is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child under 13 has created a ReelHub account, please contact us immediately. We will delete the account and all associated data.

Users between 13 and 18 years old should have parental consent before using ReelHub.

11. Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal tracking technologies:

  • Local Storage: To remember your preferences (dark mode, settings, session)
  • Session Storage: To keep you logged in during your visit
  • No Third-Party Cookies: We do not use advertising or tracking cookies

You can clear these at any time through your browser settings, but you may need to log in again.

12. Third-Party Links

ReelHub may contain links to external websites or services that are not operated by us. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. We strongly recommend reading their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

13. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own (such as servers in the United States or European Union). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

14. Content Moderation

To keep ReelHub a safe space for everyone, we may:

  • Remove content that violates our Terms of Service

We do not actively monitor all content. Our moderation is primarily reactive based on user reports.
